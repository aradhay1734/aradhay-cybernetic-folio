# Aradhay's Cyber Hub

<portfolio_prompt>

Create a modern, professional, technology-focused personal portfolio website for Aradhay Sharda, a Bachelor of Computer Science student at the University of New Brunswick (UNB), Fredericton, with an expected graduation year of 2029.

1. Overall Profile & Introduction

Aradhay Sharda is a Computer Science student with a strong interest in cybersecurity and a clear goal of building a career in the cybersecurity field. He is highly eager to learn, develop practical technical skills, and continuously improve.

He has a combination of technical education, retail/customer-service experience, strategic thinking developed through chess, and a strong willingness to take on new challenges.

The website should present Aradhay as:

An aspiring cybersecurity professional

A Computer Science student

A curious and motivated learner

A problem solver and strategic thinker

Someone with strong customer-service and communication skills

Someone who is adaptable and comfortable learning new skills

Include a professional profile photo placeholder on the Home/Hero section. The photo will be added later.

2. Visual Style

Use a modern tech-focused cybersecurity aesthetic.

Design direction:

Dark background, preferably near-black or dark navy

Blue/cyan accent colors

Clean modern typography

Subtle glowing effects

Minimal but polished animations

Subtle code/terminal-inspired visual elements

Abstract cybersecurity/network graphics

Modern cards with slight borders or glassmorphism

Smooth scrolling

Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile

Avoid making the website look like a stereotypical "hacker" website

Keep the overall appearance professional enough for recruiters and employers

The design should communicate cybersecurity + Computer Science + professionalism, rather than looking like a gaming website.

Use subtle animated elements such as:

Moving network nodes

Faint grid backgrounds

Typing/code animations

Hover effects

Smooth section transitions

Interactive project cards

Animations should remain subtle and should not distract from the content.

3. Navigation

Create a clean sticky navigation bar with:

Home

About

Skills

Experience

Projects

Education

Career Goals

Contact

Include a prominent "View My Projects" or "Explore My Work" button.

Also include a downloadable resume/CV button if a resume is added later.

4. Home / Hero Section

Create a strong first impression.

Main heading:

Aradhay Sharda

Subtitle:

Computer Science Student | Aspiring Cybersecurity Professional

Short introduction:

"Computer Science student at the University of New Brunswick with a strong interest in cybersecurity, problem-solving, and technology. Passionate about learning, building practical skills, and developing a career in cybersecurity."

Include two primary buttons:

Explore My Work

Contact Me

Include the professional profile photo placeholder on one side of the hero section.

Add subtle cybersecurity-inspired background graphics, such as a network/grid pattern.

A small status element could say:

Currently studying Computer Science at UNB

or

Aspiring Cybersecurity Professional

5. About Me Section

Create a personal but professional About section.

Explain that Aradhay is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Science at the University of New Brunswick, expected to graduate in 2029.

Highlight his interest in cybersecurity and his eagerness to learn.

Mention that his experience is not limited to academics. He has developed strong communication, customer-service, teamwork, adaptability, and problem-solving skills through working in retail environments.

Also introduce chess as an important personal interest.

Chess should be presented as more than a hobby. Explain that playing chess has helped develop:

Strategic thinking

Pattern recognition

Patience

Planning ahead

Decision-making

Analytical thinking

Learning from mist
